- **Riders pass each other and wait two abreast** (`web/arail/sims/cycling.js`): on the road and on a bike lane a rider behind a slower one pulls out to the left where there is room and no car comes from behind, passes, and goes back to the curb side; at a red light or before people who cross, riders stand beside each other. Bikes used to queue in single file, each waiting for the one ahead to get away: in Bf Neustadt's morning the riders now stand 38 % of the time instead of 52 %, the cars behind them 51 % instead of 58 %.
