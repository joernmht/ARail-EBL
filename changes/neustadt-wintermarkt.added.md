- **The Wintermarkt in Bf Neustadt** ([The Wintermarkt](docs/market.md)): the app's own layout has the Christmas market too, on the Bahnhofsplatz in front of the station, between the station and Bahnhofstraße, with its gates on Bahnhofstraße and at the station's doors. Its modules *Wintermarkt* and *Market visitors* are on by default (Examples → Sandbox switches them off), also in a browser that had saved its choice of modules before: a module on by default that a layout gains later is added to a saved choice once (layers carry `since`, the date they came, for choices saved before this release), and the modules switched off by hand stay off. The situation *Christmas market* in Settings → Simulation can now be chosen there. A layout saved with your own edits keeps its own modules.
- **The market's season**: a Christmas market is there from 26 November to 23 December (the market simulation's `from` and `until`, `MM-DD`; the layout check reports what is no date); on a date outside it the square is empty and nobody comes, also on the lab example (a situation like *Spring Sunday* shows the Marktplatz empty). Without a date every day is a market day, as before. It opens at 10:00, after Bf Neustadt's morning stories.
