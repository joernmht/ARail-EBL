- **Whose minutes? A mission for the train dispatcher** (`web/game/missions/whose-minutes.json`, `web/arail/game/dispatcher.js`, `web/game/dispatch.js`): the morning at Bf Neustadt from the desk above the signal box, on the lab's table with the computer's signaller. The trains belong to three undertakings, three of them come late, and how late is a forecast that may be off by minutes. You order who waits for whom and how long (a connection kept, or a freight train that lets the late intercity pass) and which track a train takes, and the signaller's trains do it on the table. Every minute you give one train another pays: each undertaking's goodwill falls with the minutes added to its trains, below a mark its dispatcher phones (explain, promise a clear run, or say sorry), and below another it writes to the regulator, whose verdict depends on whether your orders kept the rules of the line. The dispatcher's picture is a chart of the platform tracks over the morning: where each train is planned and where it is expected now ([the dispatcher's trains on the table](docs/games.md#the-shift-on-the-table), [the role games so far](docs/game-core.md#the-role-games-so-far)).
- **A decision can be a number** (`web/arail/game/core.js`, `web/game/play/play.js`): a game asks how much instead of which (`ask` with a `scale`: its ends, its step, its unit, where it stands), alone or with options beside it; the play page shows a slider with the number it stands at and a button that takes it (Enter in the slider does the same) ([a role game](docs/game-core.md#a-role-game)).
- **The signaller takes a dispatcher's orders** (`web/arail/game/signaller.js`): a train waits for another at most so many minutes beyond its own time, for its passengers or to let it go first (a freight train then no longer waits for a late train planned before it), and a train that is still to come takes another platform track. A mission can let a train come earlier or later than it was announced (`spread`): the forecast is told first, the true figure when the train is on its way ([the signaller of Bf Neustadt](docs/games.md#the-signaller-of-bf-neustadt)).
- **How to develop the game** ([docs/mission-design.md](docs/mission-design.md)): what every mission needs (a clear educational aim, unclear decisions, results that are not certain, roles that react and ask for compromise, choices and numbers to decide, a part that is played, the layout's world in it), shown on *Whose minutes?*, and the steps from the idea to the pull request.
