- **Operations, a case on both sides** ([#191](https://github.com/joernmht/ARail-EBL/issues/191), `web/arail/ops/plan.js`, `web/app/plan.js`): Bf Neustadt's new module **Operations** has the trains of a morning (S 1, RB 71, RE 2) with the people they are expected to carry. Railway undertaking → **Operations** puts a unit and a driver on each train, Infrastructure → **Operations** puts each train on a track; what does not work shows at once (too few places, a unit or driver still out, outside the shift; a track still taken, a train too long for the platform), with a chart over the hour. **Run** plays the plan on the layout: the trains come to their tracks with their units and drivers, late where they wait, cancelled without a unit, driver or track, full where the places are too few; then how it went. The simulation `ops-plan` keeps the plan in the layout file. The module with the breakdown story is now called **Breakdown**; a person who cannot get on a full train is counted once.
