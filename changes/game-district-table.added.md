- **The maintenance dispatcher's shift is played on the table** ([Games](docs/games.md#the-shift-on-the-table); `web/game/district.js`, `web/game/live.js`, the play page): the district of *Fault Night* lies on the lab's layout of Bf Neustadt. The maintenance base stands behind the station with a van for every team; a fault is a ring with a lamp at its asset (red and pulsing for a danger nobody guards, orange while it waits, Türkis while a team is at it); a van drives the layout's streets to it, its people stand and work at the asset, and a track asked or closed for them is a band on its rails. A click on a fault and then on a van sends the team (or the van first, then the fault); a click on the base calls a technician. Nothing is written on the table: the district's map stands small beside it. On a photo of the lab or through the camera in the lab it is augmented reality, as the signaller's shift is.
- **The games of one shift share the table**: in *A morning at Bf Neustadt* the signaller sees the dispatcher's vans come to the failed signal, and the dispatcher the trains that wait for it; a seat whose game has no board yet watches the table of the others. A board per game is named in `BOARDS` (`web/game/live.js`).
- The district's map can be played as the table is: a free team has the faults it can go to as its moves, a team that secures a place can be called off there, and the base calls the technicians on call. A move that needs a second thing of the picture names it (`target`).
