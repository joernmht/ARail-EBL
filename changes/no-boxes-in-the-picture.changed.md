- **No information boxes in the game's picture** ([Games](docs/games.md#how-a-game-looks)): information stands in the line at the top or in the rail at the side, and what is about something in the game's picture pulses on it. A new message of a mission comes into the line at the top, below the figures, instead of a box at the picture's upper right. The live backdrop draws no labels (`View` takes `labels: false`), and the game's still pictures are taken again without labels and without the numbered marker plates (`tools/game-places.mjs`); the district's picture is the lab's photo instead of the AR screenshot. The play page's browser tests check every state of a mission for text in the picture, and the Node tests that the backdrop draws no labels.
