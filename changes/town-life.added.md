- **Dead ends** (`road`: `dead_end`, `turning`, `turning_m`; `web/arail/objects/road.js`, `web/arail/core/network.js`): a street may end inside the layout on one side or both, with a turning circle (Wendekreis) or hammer (Wendehammer) of 8–40 m and the sign *Sackgasse* (Zeichen 357) where it begins. Vehicles and buses turn there; cars no longer appear and vanish at that end as at a free end. The builder's Problems tab offers *Make it a dead end* for a street that ends inside the layout. See [Dead ends](docs/streets-and-buses.md#dead-ends).
- **Pedestrian zones** (`road` kind `pedestrian`, `web/arail/objects/street-profile.js`): a paved street without cars (Fußgängerzone, Zeichen 242.1), 8–20 m wide, bikes allowed at walking pace or not, trams may run through it on one or two tracks in the paving. The builder has a tool *Pedestrian zone*. See [Pedestrian zones](docs/streets-and-buses.md#pedestrian-zones).
- **Shops on the ground floor** (`shops`: `none`, `some`, `all` of `building`, `plattenbau`, `altbau-block` and `office`; `web/arail/objects/building-kit.js`): shop windows with a sign band and awnings, lit in the shops' opening hours. Their jobs and shop visits count in the town simulation on top of the building's residents or jobs, and the building's card says how many shops it has.
- **Neustadt comes to life** (`web/layouts/ebl-neustadt.json`): in front an old town with its pedestrian zones Marktstraße, Lange Straße and the Markt, the Rathaus, St. Marien, gabled houses with shops and the Wallanlagen; behind the curve a high-rise estate of Plattenbauten round the shopping street *Passage*, with a school, a doctors' house, kindergartens and a sports field; in the east a garden town of single-family houses. Tram line 2 runs through the pedestrian zone to the *Markt*; bus 261 takes the estate from Neustadt Bf to its turning circle Am Wendekreis, bus 262 the garden town from the Busbahnhof to its turning circle in Drosselweg; several streets end as dead ends with turning circles or hammers.
