- **The hump yard on the table** ([Hump yard](docs/hump-yard.md#on-the-table), `web/arail/hump/object.js`, part of [#237](https://github.com/joernmht/ARail-EBL/issues/237)): the object `hump-yard` runs a hump simulation of its own and draws it where it is placed: the formation with its banks, ballast and rails along a fan of the tracks, the retarders (orange while they brake), every wagon with a band in the colour of its rolling class, the locomotive pushing, impacts too hard in red, the track numbers; its card says how the humping goes. Bf Neustadt has the module **Hump yard**: a virtual table module at the west end of the table, 45 mm lower, whose push track meets the table in line with the main line west. German texts in `web/arail/i18n/de/hump.js`.
