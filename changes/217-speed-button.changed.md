- **The speed is one button** (`web/app/speed.js`, [#217](https://github.com/joernmht/ARail-EBL/issues/217)): the upper right corner of the stage has two buttons, without the box around them: **Pause** (Orange while paused; Space as before) and the speed, which shows it (*10×*) and goes faster with each click (1×, 2×, 5×, 10×, 30×, then 1× again; a scenario's own speed goes on to the next one above it, `nextSpeed`). The time of day is no longer in the corner: it is in Settings → Simulation. The game page shares the control.
