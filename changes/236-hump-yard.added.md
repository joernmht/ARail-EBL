- **Hump yard, simulated on its own** ([Hump yard](docs/hump-yard.md), `web/arail/hump/`, [#236](https://github.com/joernmht/ARail-EBL/issues/236)): trains broken up over a hump. A locomotive pushes the train over the crest; each cut runs free when gravity pulls it faster than the train and rolls through the main retarder, the switch zone and its group retarder into its classification track. The physics of every cut (gravity over its whole length, rolling, curve, switch and air resistance with the wind, rotating masses, retarders on the axles inside them, speed-holding units in the tracks), cuts that touch and meet (plastic impacts), the control's switches (thrown only when free and in time; otherwise the cut is misrouted) and retarders (target braking with a measured, imperfect rolling resistance, no faster than meets a cut still rolling ahead gently), summer and winter, two track designs, results per cut and per run, pulling a formed train out. The figures of hump design come from the literature, each with its source in `params.js`. `node tools/hump.mjs` humps random trains on the command line. Not yet part of a world or of the app (that is [#237](https://github.com/joernmht/ARail-EBL/issues/237)).
