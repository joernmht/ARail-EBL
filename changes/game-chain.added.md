- **What led to what**: the result of a mission now shows every fault of its shifts on the shift's time line, from the failure to the repair, each stretch of its time out of order in the colour of whose it was: the signaller's (until it was reported, until the track was closed or opened again), the dispatcher's (until a team was sent, until the closure was asked for, until the word on what to do), the road, the team's work. While a fault waited for a team it says where the teams were; a provisional repair that held for a while and failed again is seen as that; and each fault names what it did (which trains left on the substitute signal or used another track, which connection was kept or lost, how many trains a fault on the line held). The minutes are added up by whose they were. It is read from the flows alone (`Session.threads()`, `web/arail/game/chain.js`), so any role game whose flows name a fault joins in ([The game core](docs/game-core.md#what-led-to-what)).
