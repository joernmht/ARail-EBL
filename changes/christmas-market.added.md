- **A market square with an optional Wintermarkt** ([The market square and the Wintermarkt](docs/market.md)): the lab example has a market module with the Marktplatz west of the town, Lindenallee leading to it. Its module **Wintermarkt** adds a Christmas market: 22 stalls with Glühwein, Punsch, Eierpunsch, Kakao, Baumstriezel, Handbrot, Aachener Printen, Nürnberger Lebkuchen, Dresdner Stollen, Lübecker Marzipan, Marzipan, Langos, Flammlachs, Maronen, Pilze from a stall built like a steam locomotive, Strickwaren, Lederwaren, Holzkunst and Kerzen (German names in every language), each stall with a sign of its goods, arches over the entrances reading *Wintermarkt*, a big decorated Christmas tree, a ferris wheel, a carousel and a mini train, lit at night. New object types `market-square`, `market-stall`, `market-gate`, `christmas-tree`, `ferris-wheel`, `carousel` and `mini-train` (`web/arail/objects/market.js`).
- **Market visitors** (the simulation `market`, `web/arail/sims/market.js`; the lab's module **Market visitors**): people come from the houses of the town, the ends of the streets and by train (they get off at the platforms), walk the square on flow fields round the stalls, queue, buy, drink their Glühwein at the standing tables, look at the tree and take rides, and go home, those who came by train back to the station. Cards for stalls (queue, waiting time, sales and takings), rides and visitors; the Overview rates the stalls by their queues. Scenarios *Evening at the Wintermarkt* and *Closing time at the Wintermarkt*.
- **Scenarios set the time of day**: a step's `set` takes `time` (`"19:45"`), as Settings → Simulation → Set the time.
