- **People choose their mode and learn from their trips** (`web/arail/core/modes.js`, `web/arail/sims/town.js`): for a way longer than `walk_max_m` the town's people weigh walking, the bike, bus and tram and the car by what each is expected to cost them (minutes as people feel them, money as time, the weather and the dark, their age, aid and luggage, their own liking), and a logit chooses; the constants are fitted to the shares of the modes by the length of the trip of MiD 2017. A person remembers how its ways went (a jam, left behind at the stop, soaked: a bad trip costs mood and makes the mode dearer; the mode taken last is a habit), and the town remembers the same by mode and hour (`world.experience`). So who stands in a jam every morning comes to take the tram or the bike, until the streets carry those who are left. The Overview shows the modal split and what the town has learned; a person's card what was weighed and learned; the trips' CSV names the mode ([Day and night](docs/day-and-night.md#how-people-choose-their-mode-and-what-they-learn)).
- **The town's own cars and bikes are on the streets** (`web/arail/sims/traffic.js` `drive`, `web/arail/sims/cycling.js` `ride`): who chose the car drives one of the road traffic's cars from near the door to a parking space near the destination and fetches it there again; who chose the bike rides on the bike lanes and locks it to a lamp or stand (to the station too). A car or a bike that is out comes along on the next way and is brought home.
- **Households and people who go together** (`together`, `web/arail/sims/town.js`): the people of a house live in families, couples and singles that share their cars; a child younger than 10 is taken to school by an adult of the household, pupils of a house walk to school together, a partner and the children come along shopping. A group walks two abreast at the pace of its slowest, in single file where it is narrow, and waits for those who got apart at a stop.
- **Working the town's experience out ahead** (`tools/mobility-learn.mjs`, `web/arail/sims/learning.js`, the layout's `experience`): a layout is run for some days without the app and what its town has learned is kept in the layout, so the app starts where the learning ends; `--check` (and a test for Bf Neustadt) says when the layout was changed since.
- **`stretch`** (`town`): a layout's town is smaller than a real one, so its ways count as so many times as long when people choose their mode (Bf Neustadt: 3).
- **A zebra crossing to the S-Bahn stop Pirna** (`web/layouts/ebl-neustadt.json`), where the people of the estate cross Brückenstraße in the morning.
