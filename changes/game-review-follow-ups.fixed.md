- **Short notice: the layout's own weather comes back whole after the storm** (`web/game/works.js`): the board kept only the name of the weather's preset and set it again once the storm had passed, so what a layout with a fixed weather sets on top of its preset (a temperature, the wind) was lost. It keeps the weather's mode, preset and setting now and restores all of it.
- **Fit to run: the hourly status no longer hides a train that has no unit** (`web/arail/game/workshop.js`): it counted a unit already on its way out to its own train, and one whose brake test was signed without authorisation (which fleet management keeps in), as a unit for the trains still to come. The next train then seemed to have one, and the dispatcher neither said that it had none nor lost patience over it, exactly when it was in danger.
- **Whose minutes?: a track ordered after the train's route is set** (`web/arail/game/signaller.js`, `web/arail/game/dispatcher.js`): the signaller's game took the dispatcher's order of another track for a train still to come even when its entry route was set already (a signaller who sets it early). The train went where its route led, was counted on the wrong track against the signaller, and the dispatcher's chart showed the track ordered. Now the train comes where its route is set, and the dispatcher is told so and sees it on that track.
- **Whose minutes?: a second order about another train no longer undoes the first** (`web/arail/game/signaller.js`): the signaller kept one wait per train, so that an order for GZ 47 about another train replaced "GZ 47 does not wait for IC 2045", and GZ 47 waited for the intercity again. A train now keeps a wait for each train it is ordered about.
