- **Full screen fills the screen around a photo** (`web/app/app.js`, [#220](https://github.com/joernmht/ARail-EBL/issues/220)): in full screen a photo or video with a digital layer takes the screen's shape (`App.pictureAt`): on a tablet or phone held upright the photo stays at the top and the canvas goes on below it, on a wide screen the photo lies in the middle. This holds at once for a photo the app comes with (a photo of the tour with a digital layer, a layout's own photo), so that the tour goes from photo to photo without a jump, and for a clip, a file of one's own or an image of a link once the layout is found in it (`registered`; the callers say which pictures the app comes with: `known`). A still photo whose markers are not found keeps its shape, also one the app comes with (a layout from a link whose own photo does not track). The room below it (or the bands at its sides) shows the lab around it as its camera sees it: the floor (and the sky above the horizon), the real table going on from the layout's orthophoto (around a still photo), and the layout's virtual parts, e.g. the hybrid container train's harbour below the table. Taps (also the terminal's pick of a carrier on the ground), the info cards and the labels work on the larger canvas as before: the photo's camera and homography are moved into it, the pose stays the same. The live camera, a photo without a digital layer and a picture of one's own the layout finds no markers in keep their shape. Outside full screen the stage stays as it was: the band below a photo (`view.extend_below`) shows the floor, without sky or orthophoto.
