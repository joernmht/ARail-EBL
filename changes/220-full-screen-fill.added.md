- **Full screen fills the screen around a photo** (`web/app/app.js`, [#220](https://github.com/joernmht/ARail-EBL/issues/220)): in full screen a photo or video with a digital layer takes the screen's shape, the photo in the middle (`App.pictureAt`): a picture the app comes with (a photo of the tour with a digital layer, a layout's own photo or clip) at once, so that the tour goes from photo to photo without a jump, and a file of one's own or an image of a link once the layout is found in it (`registered`; the callers say which pictures the app comes with: `known`). A still photo whose markers are not found keeps its shape, also one the app comes with (a layout from a link whose own photo does not track). The bands above and below it (a tablet or phone held upright) or at its sides (a wide screen) show the lab around it as its camera sees it: the floor (and the sky above the horizon), the real table going on from the layout's orthophoto (around a still photo), and the layout's virtual parts, e.g. the hybrid container train's harbour below the table. Taps (also the terminal's pick of a carrier on the ground), the info cards and the labels work on the larger canvas as before: the photo's camera and homography are moved into it, the pose stays the same. The live camera, a photo without a digital layer and a picture of one's own the layout finds no markers in keep their shape. Outside full screen the stage stays as it was: the band below a photo (`view.extend_below`) shows the floor, without sky or orthophoto.
