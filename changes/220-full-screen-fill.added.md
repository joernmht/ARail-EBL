- **Full screen fills the screen around a photo** (`web/app/app.js`, [#220](https://github.com/joernmht/ARail-EBL/issues/220)): in full screen a photo or video with a digital layer takes the screen's shape, the photo in the middle (`App.pictureAt`). The bands above and below it (a tablet or phone held upright) or at its sides (a wide screen) show the lab around it as its camera sees it: the floor (and the sky above the horizon), the real table going on from the layout's orthophoto (around a still photo), and the layout's virtual parts, e.g. the hybrid container train's harbour below the table. Taps, the info cards and the labels work on the larger canvas as before: the photo's camera and homography are moved into it, the pose stays the same. The live camera and a photo without a digital layer keep their shape, and so does the stage outside full screen.
