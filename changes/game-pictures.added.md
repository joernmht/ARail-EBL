- **The big game shows a role's world as a picture**: a game can add a sketch to its view, plain shapes that the play page draws as they are, and what can be acted on is chosen in the picture. The signaller has the track panel of Bf Neustadt (sections in the colours of their state, a signal with its routes, a platform track to close or open, the trains at their heads), the maintenance dispatcher a map of the district (the faults at their places, each with the teams that can be sent and their minutes on the road, the vans driving). With the mouse or the keyboard; a computer's seat shows the picture only ([The game core](docs/game-core.md)).
