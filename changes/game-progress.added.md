- **Stars, ranks, badges and a logbook**: a mission passed gives one to three stars; they add up to a rank, from Passenger to Minister of transport, and what was done earns badges. The logbook (`game/logbook/`, in the game's main menu) shows them, the railway terms met as cards to turn with a quiz, the places worked at, and a sheet for every mission played (what was decided, the terms met, the questions of the debrief), to print. Kept in the browser only.
- **Railway terms to tap**: the terms of the game's glossary (Zs 1, LuFV, the asset responsible, a qualified block, …; `web/game/glossary.js`) are marked where a question or a message names them; a tap says what the term is, in a line or two, with its German word.
- **Two more missions**: *Your first shift*, the signal box of Bf Neustadt with nothing going wrong, where the train dispatcher talks a beginner through the panel, and *Rush hour*, the same box on a bad morning (late trains, a third freight train, the signal failing earlier). The missions are in the order to play them, and a mission passed leads on to the next.
- **Missions tell a story**: a mission's `story` (in its JSON) is what people say to the players at a moment of a phase; every mission opens with somebody speaking, and goes on in later rounds.
- **A tool for the pictures**: `node tools/game-places.mjs` takes the game's virtual close-ups again, from the camera positions it keeps.
- **Sounds**: quiet cues made by the browser (a choice, a chapter, a deadline running out, the result), switched off with one button or M.
