- **Plan documents** (`web/plan/`, `web/arail/plan/`): the infrastructure of a layout as a plan sheet, like a CAD drawing. Tracks, platforms, signals, switches and the other elements are drawn at a standard scale on ISO paper (A4 to A0), with the drawing frame and its zones, the title block, a legend and a scale bar. A plan too large for one sheet is cut into sheets. You can print a sheet at its true size, or save it as SVG, as DXF for CAD, or as data. See [Plan documents](docs/plan-documents.md).
- **rail-infra/1**, an exchange format for railway infrastructure that ARail shares with the EBL's digital twin. It has tracks by their name in the control system, elements at a kilometre on their track, operating points by their abbreviation, and one vocabulary of element types. ARail writes it from a layout. The twin writes the EBL's track plan and hall plan in it, which the plan documents open. See [rail-infra/1](docs/rail-infra-format.md).
