- **Road traffic no longer comes to a standstill** (`web/arail/core/stop-signals.js`, `web/arail/core/transit.js`): in Bf Neustadt every car stood by late morning. A bus that turned out of the bus station behind the stop line of a tram stop's lights was held on the strip by those lights for good, so the people could not cross, the tram never opened its doors and the lights stayed red: a vehicle past the stop line now drives on, the strip is kept clear while the traffic stands at its far end, and vehicles that stand on it are held while the people walk between them. Every vehicle waited while any other was in a junction (the one ahead of it too), so a junction let through one vehicle at a time. Queues round a block that waited for each other's room waited for ever: one now drives into its junction. Cars pass a bus that stands at its stop when the other lane is free.
- **People no longer walk through vehicles and each other** (`web/arail/core/walking.js`): they crossed the streets wherever their walk did without looking, and beside a street without sidewalks they walked where a bus is as wide as its lane.
