- **The game shows where you work**: every role game and every mission has a place on the lab's layout (`web/game/places.js`), shown as a close-up photo with its name on the game's pages: in the head of the play, signaller, career and roles pages, on every mission's card, on every seat to choose and in every seat of a mission. The close-ups (`web/game/places/`) are cut from the lab's photos or, where everything in view is virtual, taken in the flyover. A mission says what it is in a line or two (its `hook`, at most 110 characters) with its facts as chips (roles, rounds, the time on the clock, solo or together); the long text is its briefing, opened when asked for. Missions name their `place` and `hook` in their JSON.
- **Choosing a mission looks like a game**: the missions are numbered levels on the board's dark blue, each a picture card with "Take a seat".
