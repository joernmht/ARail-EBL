- **The game shows where you work**: every role game and every mission has a place on the lab's layout (`web/game/places.js`), shown as a photo with its name on the game's pages: in the head of the play, signaller, career and roles pages, on every mission's card and in every seat of a mission. A mission says what it is in a line or two (its `hook`, at most 110 characters) with its facts as chips (roles, rounds, the time on the clock, solo or together); the long text is its briefing, opened when asked for. Missions name their `place` and `hook` in their JSON.
