- **Interaction lists scenarios to start** (`web/app/interaction.js`, `web/app/cases.js`): the side strip and the case tabs gave way to a list of cards, filtered by pills (**All**, **First steps**, **Infrastructure**, **Railway undertaking**, **Passengers**, **Freight**, **Tools**; the pill is kept). Each scenario is an example with a small task: **Start** switches on what it needs (its module, the example that has it, or the example it plays in), shows its place on the stage (the flyover over the station, the camera following a story, the photo of the real wagons) and starts the task, whose progress (*2 of 3*) stands above its panel; **Start again** starts it anew, another scenario shown stops it. The app's scenarios got names that say what happens: **Trains on tracks** and **Units and drivers** (the operations plan, the task: every train without a problem of its side), **A signal fails**, **A train breaks down** and **A traveller's journey** (the stories, the task: their steps), **Load the hybrid train** (the loading task at the hybrid container train: Start opens that example with its module *Loading task* and its photo, where the camera finds the real wagons by their tags, resets the terminal and runs the clock 10 times as fast; the task: the crane's work orders, see below). A case knows its tags, task, how-to, speed and the example it plays in (`Case.tags`, `task`, `how`, `speed`, `opens`; `start()`, `begin()`, `stop()`, `progress()`, `figures()`: the time and the moves under the progress); `App.startCase` starts one, `App.showPlace` shows a place in the flyover. Links show a scenario without starting it: `#scenarios` (the list), `#intro`, `#sandbox` (the former `#modules`), and the scenarios' ids as before.
- **The app opens with the Introduction** (`web/app/intro.js`, `web/arail/core/intro.js`): a short film of the layout, made from the layout itself (`introScenes`), so that every layout gets its own: the photo alone for what is real in the lab (real tables, tracks and platforms outlined in Türkis, the markers; `LayoutObject#real`), the photo with what the app draws (`App.fade`), then in the flyover the tracks and platforms, the signals and the interlocking, the virtual table modules, the town, the streets with their lines, the people, the terminal, the model wagons and the modules, each outlined (virtual things in Orange) with its figures, and *Your turn*. **Play** goes on by itself while the camera turns slowly; **Back**, **Next**, the list of scenes and a presenter's clicker step; the film waits while a card is open. The app's first chapter is Interaction (the game page keeps the Overview).
- **The Sandbox** (`web/app/sandbox.js`): look around freely (the flyover, the photo, the whole layout) with the module boxes, formerly Interaction → Modules, and what is shown: **Infrastructure aging**, the station's assets in the colour of their condition up to 50 years ahead without renewals (world setting `ageYears`, read by the maintenance story's `year`), the colour of people by trip purpose or mood, their walking trails and room, signs and boards, the tracks by one of their railway systems. The game page lists it too.
- **On phones the stage stays in place** (`web/app/app.css`, `pageLayout` in `web/app/ui.js`): held upright, the stage stays at the top and the panel below it scrolls on its own (keeping at least a third of the screen); held sideways (and on tablets), the stage and the panel are side by side; only short screens still scroll the page.
