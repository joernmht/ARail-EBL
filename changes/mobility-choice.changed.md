- **People on foot wait at the kerb and vehicles brake for them** (`web/arail/core/walking.js`, `world.walking`): where a walk crosses a street people look for a gap (2 s more than they need to be across; less after a long wait), cross in front of a standing vehicle but never through one (they go round a queue), and take turns with the vehicles where many cross. Vehicles brake for people on the street, also round the corner they turn. On the sidewalk people keep right when somebody comes, pass a slower one where there is room and wait beside and behind each other at a kerb; through a door they come one after the other.
- **Road traffic: `cars_per_km` are the cars of streets that flow** (`web/arail/sims/traffic.js`): every drive is planned and timed, the town learns how long drives take at each hour, and fewer cars set out where they take long (half at 2.2 times the plan, a tenth whatever happens), also by the traffic news of the moment. Bf Neustadt has 5 cars per km (it had 8).
- **Junctions: a vehicle waits only for those whose way touches its own** (`web/arail/core/transit.js` `movesConflict`): not for the one ahead, the oncoming one going straight or one turning off; who turns left waits for the oncoming traffic. Vehicles follow at the leader's speed with the way of a second between (`followSpeed`), so a queue starts off together.
- **Buses and trams run as often as the people who really take them need** (`web/arail/sims/town.js` `serviceInterval`): the share of the long ways by bus and tram is what the choice of the modes gives for their length, not a set share; a line's bus or tram on its way leaves a stop when everybody is on and off.
- **`bus_share` and `car_share`** (`town`) now only switch: 0 nobody takes the bus or tram (or the car), 1 everybody who can, in between (0.5, the default) people choose; the tastes are the table `modes`.
- **A plain street** (no sidewalks): people walk beside it on the verge, not at the edge of the carriageway (`web/arail/objects/street-profile.js`).
