- **German mode** (`game/german/`, `web/arail/game/german.js`): an endless game in which the load on the infrastructure rises until nothing moves, with a score to beat and the best one kept in the browser. In the **signal box** of Bf Neustadt a timetable that never ends gets denser and denser: every train has its planned track and its time, and needs a route in and a route out; a train through gives 10 points, 5 fewer on another track than planned and 1 fewer for every minute late; with more than three trains waiting on one line the game is over. At the **container terminal** trains bring containers, a truck comes for each of them, and one crane does every move: it is over with more than two trains or five trucks waiting, or with a yard in which no move is left. The load stops rising where careful play still keeps up, so a game played well lasts long. Space starts a game and the next one; both can be played with the keys. See [German mode](docs/games.md#german-mode).
