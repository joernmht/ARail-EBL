- **A camera bridge to print** (`hardware/camera-bridge`, [Camera bridge](docs/camera-bridge.md)): a lattice bridge over the tracks, inspired by DB Cargo's camera bridges, that holds a phone to film the lab's trains for a model that checks train compositions; a side stand that holds the phone low beside the track, at the height of the locomotives' numbers; cable troughs with lids for the charging cable. The parts are generated by a parametric script (tracks, spacing, heights, feet, cable), with ready STL files for the EBL; long slabs under the columns keep the bridge standing with the phone off the middle. `tests/python/test_camera_bridge.py` checks the parts.
