- **Video tools** ([Videos of the lab](docs/videos.md#the-project-pages-video)): `tools/video/screen.mjs` records a page of the site as it is used (clicks, a cursor); `tools/video/typewriter.mjs` types a video's lines as an old computer would. `arail-compose` takes a clip as a shot (`clip`, from `first`), holds one frame of a clip as the bare picture (`still`), tints a shot in one colour (`tint`), lays the lines over the video (`say`, `overlay`, `--cues`), mixes sounds into a sound track (`sounds`, `typing`) and writes each shot on its own (`--shots`).
