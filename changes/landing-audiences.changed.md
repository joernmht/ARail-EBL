- **The project page speaks to three groups** (`web/index.html`): a switch below the video shows a page for educators, scientists or practitioners, each with its question, three ways to use ARail, the way from an idea to something that runs, and a board of what others have brought; every application has a line for the group chosen, and `?for=scientists` opens a group's page. The page is shorter: the base and "Bring it to your lab" are one section, the applications are compact cards.
- **Three ways in** (`.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/`): issue forms for a teaching concept, a paper to implement (filed by an author and confirmed by a maintainer with the label `accepted`, it allows giving the paper to an AI that implements it; its text is not republished) and a case from practice, with the labels `teaching-concept`, `paper` and `practice-case`. What comes of them is listed on the page's boards from `web/references.json`, with its status and a link ([Contributing](CONTRIBUTING.md#the-boards-of-the-project-page)).
- **The game's main menu has at most five choices** (`web/game/title.js`): Continue, Games (Missions, Signaller, Free play), Career (Logbook, Career planner, Roles), Options and Leave; Back, Escape and the left arrow go back one menu.
