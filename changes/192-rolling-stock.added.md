- **Trains as they are** ([#192](https://github.com/joernmht/ARail-EBL/issues/192), `web/arail/rail/stock.js`, `web/arail/rail/draw.js`): the trains at the platforms are drawn solid from their vehicles, as long as they are, on their track: the cars of a Talent 2 with cabs and pantograph, a BR 146 with double-deck coaches, doors open on the platform's side. They stop with their front near the end of the platform and come in and leave along the track. Without a consist of the layout a train runs with the **default fleet** of its line (RE: BR 146 and four DBpza, RB: a two-car ET 442, the new `et442s`, S: a four-car ET 442); in the rush hours units run coupled where the platform is long enough (`peak` in a layout's `consists`). Vehicles have **places** (seats and standing): a train's card shows them and the people on board; a full train takes nobody else, and the people left behind are counted. The units of the operations story are drawn the same way.
