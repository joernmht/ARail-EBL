- **A town on a ChArUco board** (`web/layouts/charuco-town.json`, `web/markers/charuco-town-a3.pdf`, [Live AR on XR glasses](docs/live-ar.md#a-town-on-a-charuco-board)): a table demo without the lab. A ChArUco board for A3 (7 × 5 squares of 56 mm) whose 17 markers (ArUco Original, IDs 0–16, 42 mm) are the layout's locked marker map, so nothing needs surveying; on it a town at 1:250 with a ring street and a one-way tram, a bus line, a Plattenbau, Altbau blocks, a supermarket, residents and cars. Open it with `app/?layout=../layouts/charuco-town.json&camera=1` or in ARail Live.
