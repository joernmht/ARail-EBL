- **The Striezelmarkt in Bf Neustadt** ([The Striezelmarkt](docs/market.md)): the app's own layout has the Christmas market too, on the Festplatz am Wall, a table module of its own behind St. Marien with its gates on the Wallanlagen. Its modules *Striezelmarkt* and *Market visitors* are on by default (Examples → Sandbox switches them off), also in a browser that had saved its choice of modules before: a module on by default that a layout gains later is added to a saved choice once. The situation *Christmas market* in Settings → Simulation can now be chosen there. A layout saved with your own edits keeps its own modules.
- **The market's season**: a Christmas market is there from 26 November to 23 December (the market simulation's `from` and `until`); on a date outside it the square is empty and nobody comes. Without a date every day is a market day, as before. It opens at 10:00, after Bf Neustadt's morning stories.
