- **The Striezelmarkt in Bf Neustadt** ([The Striezelmarkt](docs/market.md)): the app's own layout has the Christmas market too, on the Festplatz am Wall, a table module of its own behind St. Marien with its gates on the Wallanlagen. Its modules *Striezelmarkt* and *Market visitors* are on by default (Examples → Sandbox switches them off), and the situation *Christmas market* in Settings → Simulation can now be chosen there.
