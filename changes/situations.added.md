- **Situations** (`web/app/situations.js`, Settings → Simulation): characteristic combinations of a day with its weekday, a time, the weather and the passenger demand, set in one click: *Winter weekday, morning rush*, *Foggy Monday in November*, *Spring Sunday*, *Thunderstorm on an autumn night*, *Hot weekend in the summer of 2050* (39 °C), and the events *Christmas market* (switches on the modules Wintermarkt and Market visitors where the layout has them) and *After the football match* (a crowd surge at every stop; a stadium is planned in [#264](https://github.com/joernmht/ARail-EBL/issues/264)). Time of day, season, weather and demand one by one are folded away under *Each on its own*.
- **Weekends** (`core/clock.js` `WEEKEND_PROFILES`, `Clock#weekend`): with a date on a Saturday or Sunday, trains, buses, cars and random passengers follow a weekend's profile, without a rush hour (table in [Day and night](docs/day-and-night.md#demand-over-the-day)). Without a date every day stays a working day.
