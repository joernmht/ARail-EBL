- **The last wagon: a mission for the yard master, the first of the freight roles** (`web/game/missions/last-wagon.json`, `web/arail/game/yard.js`, `web/game/yard.js`): the first half hour of an early shift at the hump yard of the lab's table, in the frost. Three trains stand in the arrival tracks, a fourth comes late by a forecast; four trains go out, each from its own classification track at its cut-off, and a booked wagon that is not in then stays behind. You say which train goes over the hump next and how fast it is pushed (and change the speed while it is pushed: tap the hump), how long an outbound train waits for its last wagons (three minutes its path forgives), and what the one locomotive does besides pushing: take the wagon that must not be humped round to its track, fetch a cut from the wrong track, close up a track. The hump is ARail's hump simulation, so pushing faster really makes good runners catch up bad ones: a switch cannot be thrown between them, wagons meet too hard. The hump operator counts the impacts, the production dispatcher the minutes, the customer service agent the wagons left behind; each phones, asks for a promise that another pays, and acts at the end of their patience ([the hump yard on the table](docs/games.md#the-shift-on-the-table), [the role games so far](docs/game-core.md#the-role-games-so-far)).
- **The hump yard shows a game's run, and its locomotive moves cuts** (`web/arail/hump/object.js`, `web/arail/hump/simulation.js`): the layout's hump yard can draw a hump simulation that runs elsewhere instead of its own (`show`); the simulation lets a locomotive take a standing cut to another track (`moveCut`) and set wagons into a track without humping them (`setOut`) ([in the game](docs/hump-yard.md#in-the-game-the-last-wagon)).
- **A game's table can be another part of the layout** (`web/game/live.js`): a board names the modules of the layout that are on with it, where the camera may look and what the parts of its picture are called; the yard master's table is the module Hump yard, west of the station.
