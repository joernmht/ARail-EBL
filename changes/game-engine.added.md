- **The game engine: role games wired by flows, in turns and shifts** (`web/arail/game/`, [The game core](docs/game-core.md)): the game core gets **ports** (the flows a role's game takes from other roles and gives them: a fault report, a repair status, a track closure, a budget; `flows.js` holds 70 flows with their German terms, and the parties of the railway), **moves by name** (`Game.act`, kept so that a game can be played again), a **view** from which a page can draw a game it does not know, and **computer players** (`static player`). A **session** (`session.js`) seats several role games, each played by a person or by the computer, wires them by their ports and plays slow roles (days to years) in turns and fast roles (seconds to hours) in shifts on one clock; a turn's decisions reach the shift as state, the shift's reports the next turn. Flows travel at tick boundaries and the people's moves are recorded, so a session can be saved and played again (`Session.save`, `Session.replay`). **Missions are data** (`mission.js`, format `arail-mission/1`, `web/game/missions/`): seats, phases, rounds, conditions, injects, briefing and debrief; `validateMission` names what is wrong, `testRun` plays a mission with the computer on every seat.
- **The maintenance dispatcher's game, Fault Night** (`web/arail/game/maintenance.js`): a shift in the maintenance control centre of the district around Bf Neustadt: faults by their report paths, one or two emergency teams and a technician on call, the closure asked of the signaller before a team works in the track. With the signaller's game in one session (the mission `neustadt-morning`), the signaller's fault report sends the dispatcher's team, and the repair time is in another player's hands.
- **The signaller's game in a session**: its fault report, track closures, delays and the shift's account go out as flows; the repair team, a closure request and a request to hold a train for a connection come in; the condition of the assets decides whether the signal fails. A computer's signaller (`SignallerAutopilot`) plays the seat nobody takes.
