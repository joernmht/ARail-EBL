- **Transportleitung is played on the table** ([Games](docs/games.md#the-shift-on-the-table); `web/game/control.js`, the mission `transportleitung`): the undertaking's operations control works on the table of the signaller's game that runs the same morning (the signaller's seat is the computer's). Its regional trains have a ring where they are; one pulses orange while its passengers have not been told of a delay, and in the choice's colour while a question is about it: the late feeder out on the line and the train at platform 2 that could wait for it. A click on a train chooses it, and telling its passengers is its move. A hold that is asked for is kept by the signaller: the train stands at the platform until the changers have crossed.
- The operations control has **a picture of its connections** (`TransportControlGame`'s sketch): a row for each (the feeder, the passengers who change, the train they change to; green once it is kept, red once it is missed), the trains cancelled later in the day and the reserves.
- A mission can say which of the signaller's trains come late (`options.late` of the seat: a train's id and its seconds), whatever the level.
