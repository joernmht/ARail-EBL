- **ARail Live: live AR on XR glasses** (`web/live/`, [Live AR on XR glasses](docs/live-ar.md)): a page of its own for XR glasses (such as the VITURE Luma Ultra) and phones, reduced to the layout's town and its moving cars, and a performance test. The town is drawn once into a picture and fitted to every new camera pose by the homography of a plane parallel to the table (exact for a camera that turns; drawn again when a moving camera's parallax exceeds 2 px, the image leaves the picture or the town changes), applied by the browser as a CSS `matrix3d`; cars are drawn in every frame with the buildings that may hide them cut out; markers are found in a worker. Camera image or black (see-through glasses), a fit of the view to the glasses, a demo without a camera, the lab's clip, settings in the address and numbers in the corner that compare it with drawing everything in every frame. `World.draw(view, {objects, moving})` draws a part of the world.
