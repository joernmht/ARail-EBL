- **Fit to run: a mission for the workshop manager, the first of the rolling stock roles** (`web/game/missions/fit-to-run.json`, `web/arail/game/workshop.js`, `web/game/workshop.js`): a night in the hall of Betriebswerk Neustadt, 22:00 to 05:24 in 37 minutes, as maintenance delivery among the four functions of the entity in charge of maintenance (ECM). Four units come in with their orders (brake pads, a door's drive, a wheel flat, an inspection), four trains want a unit from 04:24. You say which unit stands over the pit, on the lifting jacks and on the wheel lathe, and release to service what is done. What is not yours to decide is the lesson: a finding beyond the order is fleet management's (phone and wait while the pit stands, repair it under the standing order, or write it into the release), a limit is maintenance development's (measure again, change the part, ask for a concession, or take the reading), and a unit released to service runs only once fleet management has returned it to operation. How deep the lathe cuts and what time the operator's dispatcher is given are numbers. The fleet manager, the maintenance development engineer and the dispatcher count differently; each phones, asks for a promise that another pays, and acts at the end of their patience ([The workshop hall](docs/workshop.md), [the role games so far](docs/game-core.md#the-role-games-so-far)).
- **A workshop hall seen inside, as a module of Bf Neustadt** (`web/arail/ops/workshop.js`, `web/layouts/ebl-neustadt.json`): the object `workshop-hall` is a hall without its roof, its walls drawn from within: the inspection pit with roof platforms, the lifting jacks, the underfloor wheel lathe, a crane, three stabling tracks and the throat every move goes through. It is lit at night. By itself it shows a quiet night; a game's night is shown in it (`show`): units on their way through the throat, raised on the jacks, fitters, the lathe cutting, a lamp at each track in the colour of its unit's state ([layout format](docs/layout-format.md#workshop-hall-a-workshop-hall-seen-inside)).
- **A shift can run faster than the clock** (`web/arail/game/mission.js`, `web/arail/game/core.js`, `web/game/play/play.js`): a mission's shift has a `pace`, the seconds of the day per second of the shift (1 by default; the night in the hall has 12). The page shows the time of day and the table's night by it; a game that runs at another pace says so in `static pace`, and a mission that differs is not sound ([missions as data](docs/game-core.md#missions-as-data)).
