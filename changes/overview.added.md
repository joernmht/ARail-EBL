- **The Overview tab** (`web/app/overview.js`, `web/arail/core/states.js`): the modules are switched on and off here, and it rates the state of what the simulations keep (`Simulation.states()`): assets of the infrastructure by their condition, stops, people waiting and residents by their mood, units of the rail operations by their wear. One figure gives the mean of all kinds; the worst and the best kind are shown with their mean and a small histogram on fixed axes (all kinds on request), and a search finds assets and people by name or kind, the worst first, with their info card a click away.
