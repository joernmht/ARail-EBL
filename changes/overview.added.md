- **The Overview tab** (`web/app/overview.js`, `web/arail/core/states.js`): the modules are switched on and off here, and it rates the state of what the simulations keep (`Simulation.states()`): assets of the infrastructure by their condition, stops by their load (people waiting against 2 people per m²) and mood, people waiting and residents by their mood, units of the rail operations by their wear. One figure gives the mean of all kinds; the worst and the best kind are shown with their mean and a small histogram (all kinds on request), and a search finds assets and people by name or kind, the worst first, with their info card a click away.
