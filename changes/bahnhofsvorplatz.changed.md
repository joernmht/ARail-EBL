- **Bf Neustadt gets a Bahnhofsplatz** ([Streets and buses](docs/streets-and-buses.md), `web/layouts/ebl-neustadt.json`): Bahnhofstraße moves about 29 m away from the tracks (it swings away from Ringstraße in an S-curve of 26 m), and the station building is 13 m deep instead of 7 m. Between the station and the street lies the Bahnhofsplatz: the square of the Christmas market, the bus station (ZOB, three bays along the street, lines 261, 262 and the express bus 270, a footpath up to it from Bahnhofstraße) with four low shops behind its bus lane and bike stands for Bike+Ride, the P+R, a Fahrradstation with bike stands and a kiosk. Everything on it is one floor with a flat roof, so the tracks stay in view. The front row of houses on Bahnhofstraße, the WBS 70 slab and the old bus station across the street make room for it; the Hotel am Bahnhof and the Altbau now stand across Bahnhofstraße. Ada's journey starts in Lange Straße 29.
- **The Striezelmarkt on the Bahnhofsplatz** ([The market square and the Striezelmarkt](docs/market.md)): its stalls stand in two rows along the station's front, the tree, the ferris wheel, the carousel, the mini train and the locomotive stall beside the station; its arches stand at the gates on Bahnhofstraße and at the station's doors.
- **Tram line 2 turns Am Wall, no longer round St. Marien**: the church stands beside the track on its Kirchplatz, the track crosses Wallstraße (now one street, without its two turning circles) to a loop on a green of its own on the module Am Wall, where the Festplatz was. The Busbahnhof's loop lies further south, so that its neck keeps room for curves of 25 m.
