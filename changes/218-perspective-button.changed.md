- **One perspective button** (`web/app/tour-nav.js`, [#218](https://github.com/joernmht/ARail-EBL/issues/218)): the photo tour's six perspective buttons gave way to one in the upper left corner of the stage. Each click looks at the part from its next side round the table (front, front right, back right, back, back left, front left; `ROUND`), only the sides it has a photo from (while a scenario runs, only its photos); its icon shows where the camera stands (the table from above, the camera with its view), its name the side shown (*Perspective: From the front left*), its title where a click goes. With no other side to go to it is off. A second click while a photo loads goes on from that photo. The arrows at the edges stay as they are.
