- **The game page opens like a game** (`web/game/title.js`): called without a layout or a case, [game/](web/game/) shows a title screen: the lab's photos full screen, on desktop and phones, drifting slowly and fading from one to the next, with the game's name and a main menu: Continue (the game opened last), Missions (the big game), Signaller, Free play (the Sandbox, Assets, Operations, Terminal and Journeys on the lab's layout), Career, Roles, Options (English or German, the moving background, full screen) and Leave. The arrow keys, Enter and Escape work as in a game's menu; with less motion asked for by the system the photos stay still. Links with a layout or a case still open the games directly, and the bar's new Menu button leads back to the title screen. See [Games](docs/games.md#where-the-games-are).
