- **A calmer, simpler app** (`web/app/`): the stage's chips appear only when something needs attention (no markers, a photo without a digital layer, disruptions, recording); the clock with the speed moved into the bar and opens Settings → Simulation. Stops show a badge with the people waiting instead of a full board (**Departure boards at the stops** in Settings → View brings the boards back, world setting `stopBoards`). The View tab is now the first view of **Settings** (old `#view` links open it); the module boxes lose their pills and long texts (the description is the tooltip), the duplicate Flyover, Map and 2.5D buttons and most hints are gone, and the tracking figures are folded into *Tracking details*.
