- **A calmer, simpler app** (`web/app/`): the View tab is now the first view of **Settings** (Settings → View: modules, examples, what is shown, the grid, the camera, the language; old `#view` links open it), and a new **Stops** tab lists the platforms and stops with the people waiting and the next vehicles, and the town's people. The stage's chips appear only when something needs attention (no markers, a photo without a digital layer, disruptions, recording); the clock with the speed moved into the bar and opens Settings → Simulation. Stops show a badge with the people waiting instead of a full board (**Departure boards at the stops** in Settings → View brings the boards back, world setting `stopBoards`). The module boxes lose their pills and long texts (the description is the tooltip), the duplicate Flyover, Map and 2.5D buttons and most hints are gone, and the tracking figures are folded into *Tracking details*.
