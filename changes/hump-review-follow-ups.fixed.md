- **Hump yard: no wagons set before a cut on its way** (`web/arail/hump/simulation.js`, [#277](https://github.com/joernmht/ARail-EBL/issues/277)): `moveCut` and `setOut` no longer put wagons into a classification track while a cut still rolls through the switch zone towards it; they stood at the track's entrance just before that cut arrived. Before, only a cut already past the last switch counted.
- **The hump yard on the table no longer stands still** ([Hump yard](docs/hump-yard.md), `web/arail/hump/simulation.js`, [#260](https://github.com/joernmht/ARail-EBL/issues/260)): cuts held against the wagons of a track stayed held when the track was pushed up or pulled out, and with them the cuts behind them up to the crest: the train was blocked for good (pushed at 2 m/s or faster, within the hour). They stand free again now. A derailed cut is taken away by the breakdown crew (`rerail`, noted `cut.rerailed`), by itself after `rerail_s`: on the table after five minutes, so that the yard goes on.
- **Hump yard: a cut pushed into its track is no longer counted as stopped short** (`web/arail/hump/simulation.js`, [#246](https://github.com/joernmht/ARail-EBL/issues/246)): a cut that stopped across a track's entrance stands where `pushUp` puts it (in the track), and one that leaves with the track's train (`pullOut`) ended in that track.
- **Hump yard: a cut aims at the wagons where they stand now** (`web/arail/hump/simulation.js`, [#246](https://github.com/joernmht/ARail-EBL/issues/246)): when a track is pushed up or pulled out while a cut is in its group retarder on its way there, the speeds it needs are worked out again; it aimed at the rear of wagons that had left and, with target braking alone, stopped there with a gap beyond.
- **Hump yard: a run ends only when nothing would start again** (`web/arail/hump/simulation.js`, [#246](https://github.com/joernmht/ARail-EBL/issues/246)): `run()` no longer ends while a retarder is still opening or gravity would start a standing cut again on the next step.
- **Hump a train: nothing to push when the hump is blocked** (`web/app/hump.js`, [#260](https://github.com/joernmht/ARail-EBL/issues/260)): once the task is over with cuts still on the hump (the hump blocked), Slower, Faster and Stop are off, as they are once every cut is released.
