- **The simulation takes less time per step** (`web/arail/core/network.js`, `transit.js`, `math.js`, `web/arail/sims/town.js`, `traffic.js`, `cycling.js`): the same simulation, step for step, with less work for it. The town weighs a trip by bus by the lengths of the walks to the stops, which the road network keeps (`walkLength`), and looks only at the people walking for the zebra crossings and the riders; shortest-path trees and routes run over the ways out of each node worked out once; cars, bikes and buses find their junction and their neighbours with fewer searches and arrays; the effects of disruptions are one shared object while none is active. On the large example (Bf Neustadt) a step takes about 40 % less time, and the first step after loading or switching modules (the town, the traffic and the lines built) nearly half.
