- **Three chapters: Overview, Interaction, Settings** ([#188](https://github.com/joernmht/ARail-EBL/issues/188)): the panel's tabs no longer come and go with the modules. The module boxes moved from the Overview to **Interaction → Modules**; Settings → Simulation no longer lists the modules, a case whose module is off offers it instead (**Switch on “…”**, **Open the example**, **Add to this layout**).
- **The cases by side** ([#189](https://github.com/joernmht/ARail-EBL/issues/189), `web/app/interaction.js`, `web/app/cases.js`): Interaction has a strip at its side, its labels reading upwards: Modules, the sides **Infrastructure** and **Railway undertaking** (EVU) with their cases as tabs, and the tools Build and Disruptions. A case is a class (`Case`, with `StoryCase`, `TerminalCase`, `BuildCase`, `DisruptionsCase`): its side, its name, the simulation or app module it needs and its panel; a page lists its case classes (`Page.cases`). The app: Infrastructure → **Disruption** (the maintenance story, formerly the Infrastructure tab), Railway undertaking → **Disruption** (the breakdown story, formerly the Operations tab), **Terminal** and **Journeys**; the game page: Infrastructure → **Assets**, Railway undertaking → **Operations**, **Terminal** and **Journeys**. Links: `#modules`, `#infra-disruption`, `#evu-disruption`, `#terminal`, `#journeys`, `#build`, `#disrupt`; the former `#ops` and `#infra` open their story. The scrolling tables of the app (crane jobs, model wagons, the marker map, the control system's trains) are regions the keyboard reaches.
- **Terminal is a module of Bf Neustadt** ([#190](https://github.com/joernmht/ARail-EBL/issues/190)): the terminal simulation and its scenario *Morning shift at the terminal* are in the layer `terminal` of `ebl-neustadt.json` (off until switched on); the examples *terminal at the crane* and, on the game page, *Bf Neustadt and its terminal* switch it on.
