- **Passenger mobility: one person, different characteristics** (`web/arail/core/mobility.js`, #165): every person of the simulations (at the stops, the town's residents, the journeys' travellers, the people in the cars) has a profile of characteristics: age (child, adult, old), a walking aid (walking stick, rollator, wheelchair) or a pram, luggage (light, heavy, bulky), a driving licence and a car. It is drawn from the person's own seeded stream; the shares and every effect are one table of the passenger simulation for the whole layout, `people` (a row per trait, a cell per effect, each with a default and its source; a layout sets only the cells it changes, and the layout check names rows and cells it does not know; see [Day and night](docs/day-and-night.md#passenger-mobility)).
- **Walking speed from a normal distribution** (#166): a base speed of N(1.34 m/s, 0.26 m/s) cut to 0.5–2.2 m/s (Weidmann 1993; `rng.truncNormal`, `normalCdf`, `normalQuantile` in `core/math.js`) times the factors of the traits; the passengers, the town and the journeys' planner walk at it instead of their own constants.
- **Boarding by characteristics** (#167): platforms have a height (`height_m`, 0.76 m) and may have staff who help wheelchair users (`assistance`), bus stops and terminals a kerb height (`kerb_m`, 0.18 m); vehicles a floor height at the doors, a ramp and a door for the multi-purpose area. People go through a door one after the other at their own pace (much slower with a step for old people and with a rollator or a pram); a wheelchair user gets on only at level boarding or with help, else is left behind (event `passenger.denied`); the doors stay open while somebody is in them, so the dwell follows the boarding. A stop's card shows the step, the help, the boarding times and who was left behind.
- **Luggage** (#168): heavy luggage grows with the length of the trip; it slows walking, takes loading time at the doors and room in the vehicle (none in a train's racks).
- **Driving licence and car** (#169): in the town, those with a licence and a car drive their long ways (`car_share`, not drawn: the walk to the car, loading, the drive, parking); the others are captive users. The drivers of the road traffic have a licence, and the people of a car walk at the slowest one's pace and take time to load and unload.
- **Space factor** (#170): vehicles hold spaces, not heads (seats and standing room; a bus 76, a tram 180, a train car 120, with wheelchair places); a pram, a wheelchair or a suitcase takes more room, squeezing costs mood, and a full vehicle leaves people behind. A vehicle's card shows its load; the Overview lists the vehicles at the stops by the room left.
- **Door-to-door travel times** (#171): the town's trips and the journeys' travellers are recorded from door to door in their parts (on foot, waiting, at the doors, riding, by car or transfer), planned and real; Settings → Simulation → Town shows them by group (old people, wheelchair, pram, luggage, no licence …) with a CSV. A journeys traveller gets the age, aid and luggage its maker chooses, and the planner plans with them (a wheelchair user only gets plans with level boarding or help).
- **The room people take** (#172, Settings → View, setting `spaceCircles`): a circle on the ground whose area is a person's space, and the load of each vehicle at a stop in spaces; people are drawn with their pram, rollator, stick, wheelchair or luggage.
