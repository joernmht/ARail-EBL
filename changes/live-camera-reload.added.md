- **ARail Live: Reload next to the camera** (`web/live/`, [Live AR on XR glasses](docs/live-ar.md#with-xr-glasses-viture-luma-ultra-and-others)): Settings → Camera shows while the source is the camera, also with only one camera, and **Reload** looks for cameras again and starts the one chosen. The list was made only when the camera started, so a camera plugged in or woken later (the glasses' own) never appeared. When the camera chosen is not there (unplugged, asleep or used by another program), another one runs and the page says so.
