- **Waldhof in the photo tour and the RB 30 to Neustadt** (`web/layouts/ebl-neustadt.json`, module *Waldhof*): the layout of Bf Neustadt now also covers Waldhof south and Waldhof (marker groups S10 and S11). Their markers were placed from the photos of 9 October 2026, so twelve of their photos open with the digital layer in the photo tour and are tracked: the virtual world goes on from Bf Neustadt towards Waldhof. The module has Waldhof's tracks 1, 2, 4 and 5 with its two platforms, the NBS on both laps, and the RB 30 from Waldhof to Neustadt, which runs out of view round the hall and turns at Bf Neustadt, track G2. See [Lab session](docs/lab-session.md#waldhof-the-layout-extended-along-the-window-side).
- **Rail lines** (`rail-line`, `web/arail/rail/lines.js`): trains that run along a chain of tracks from platform to platform, out of view where the tracks do not meet, stop at every platform in both directions, turn at the last one and run back; a platform that lists only the line is its own, at the others the timetable runs as well (never as the line) and the board shows both; the tracks between two gaps are one track for both directions, and a train keeps its distance to the one ahead. Build → Transport → Rail line: tap the tracks in order. The services send a train the other way when asked (`call(…, {direction})`), and hold back the timetable's next train while a line's train waits (`defer`). See [Rail lines](docs/rail-systems.md#rail-lines).
