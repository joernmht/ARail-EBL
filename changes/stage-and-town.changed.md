- **The time and speed in the corner of the stage** (`web/app/speed.js`): the time of day, **Pause** and the speeds **1×**, **2×**, **5×**, **10×**, **30×** are in the upper right corner of the stage, over the photo and the flyover alike and also in full screen (the speed chosen in Orange, a scenario's own too; a speed none of them has beside the time; Space pauses; the time opens Settings → Simulation). While something is placed or picked on the stage it steps aside, so that a tap in the corner reaches the picture. It replaces the clock in the bar and the speed buttons of Settings → Simulation, the operations plan and the game page's terminal.
- **Leaving a scenario takes the stage back to the town** (`App.showHome`, the page's `home`): going back to the list (or to another scenario) from a scenario that was started ends it, as before, and now the stage shows Bf Neustadt again with its photo, in the camera view, with the modules it had when the scenario was started (opened again if the scenario played in another layout, such as the hybrid container train); the Sandbox keeps the modules switched in it (`Case.keepModules`). The live camera, a photo or video one opened oneself, or a photo chosen while the town loads stays; a scenario only shown, not started, leaves the stage as it is, and another chapter (Overview, Settings) leaves a scenario running, as before. The game page has no home: its stage stays as it is.
- **While a scenario runs, the photo tour offers only the photos of its task** (`Case.photoScope`, `Case.photoParts`, `TourNav.setScope`, `PhotoTour` option `only`): those of its layout's digital layer, on the parts of the table where it happens (Bf Neustadt's four parts for the scenarios at the station, the goods yard's photos of the hybrid container train for its loading task), so that the tour no longer leads out of the scenario to another layout. The sides without such a photo are off, and with one part only the arrows are hidden. The Introduction shown at first, the Sandbox and the tools offer all photos.
- **Soft changes of picture and perspective** (`App.crossfade`, `FlyCamera.fromPose`): the stage fades from one picture to the next (another photo, the flyover and back, another layout or its modules). From a photo, a scenario's flyover starts where the photo's camera stands and flies to its place (the Introduction's scenes, a scenario's place, a story), and a story's steps are flown to in a second. With *reduce motion* set in the system the stage cuts as before.
- **The containers of work orders glow** (`drawHighlights`, `OVERLAY.task`): a container that a work order asks to move is no longer outlined in Orange, the colour of the container chosen, but glows in Gelb: the whole container filled and edged over Dunkelblau, pulsing (`View` option `pulse`, steady with *reduce motion*), with a yellow badge of its number (also in the panel's list of work orders), also one deep in a stack.
- **More people, less traffic in Bf Neustadt** (`web/layouts/ebl-neustadt.json`): the town shows all of its 25 people per 100 residents (`max_people` 3000; with the default 300 it showed one in forty, so the streets and platforms of the first picture were nearly empty), and the streets carry fewer cars and bikes (`cars_per_km` 8 instead of 18, `riders_per_km` 4). In the photo the first minutes show some forty people walking and a dozen waiting instead of five, and a handful of cars and bikes. The live page, which takes the layout's traffic, starts with fewer cars too (its slider sets more).
