- **Scenarios lighter in text** (Interaction, `web/app/interaction.js`, `story.js`, `intro.js`, `plan.js`, `terminal.js`, `sandbox.js`): a scenario shown has no boxes and no repeated description (its card in the list says what it is about): its name, Start until it is started, then the bar of its task (and the time and moves of the loading task), and only what is needed to play it. A story and the Introduction show only the step they are at, its title and text; below it the bar of their steps, one slider with a segment per step (a click or the arrow keys go there, replacing the list of all steps); then **Back**, **Next**, **Play** and **Restart** in one row; at their end *Done* beside the bar (a status screen readers say). Start opens the flyover, and while a story runs its camera always follows it (the switch *Camera follows* is gone); a photo chosen by hand stays, the story drawn on the real table. A control used before Start starts the scenario first, and the bar keeps the keyboard focus. Gone: the task box with its task and how-to lines, the side above the name, the story's name and description, the hints of the story, the film, the Sandbox, the operations plan and the terminal, and the case's second Start again (each panel has its own, now called **Restart**). In the loading task, once it is started, the terminal shows only Restart, the work orders, the containers and the move, and while no model wagon is seen the links that print their deck cards (no model wagons table: the wagons' states stay in Settings → View → Tracking details). On a layout with work orders the app offers no Save as start state, which would change the task's start. The trains of the operations plan are marked by a line at their side instead of a box.
