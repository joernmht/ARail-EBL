- **Scenarios lighter in text** (Interaction, `web/app/interaction.js`, `story.js`, `intro.js`, `plan.js`, `terminal.js`, `sandbox.js`): a scenario shown has no boxes and no repeated description (its card in the list says what it is about): its name, Start until it is started, then the bar of its task (and the time and moves of the loading task), and only what is needed to play it. A story and the Introduction show only the step they are at, its title and text; below it the bar of their steps, one slider with a segment per step (a click or the arrow keys go there, replacing the list of all steps); then **Back**, **Next**, **Play** and **Restart** in one row. While a story runs the flyover's camera always follows it (the switch *Camera follows* is gone), and a control used before Start starts the scenario first. Gone: the task box with its task and how-to lines, the side above the name, the story's name and description, the hints of the story, the film, the Sandbox, the operations plan and the terminal, and the case's second Start again (each panel has its own, now called **Restart**). In the loading task the terminal shows only Restart, the work orders, the containers and the move (no Save as start state, which would change the task, and no model wagons table; they stay in Settings → View → Tracking details). The trains of the operations plan are marked by a line at their side instead of a box.
