- **Nobody left a building for five minutes after loading** (`TownSimulation._place`, `web/arail/sims/town.js`): placing the town's people at the time of day (a layout loaded, the clock set) kept each of them inside for five clock minutes, and then everybody due meanwhile left in the same frame. They now leave when their plan says, one by one.
- **The nearest street node is kept** (`RoadNetwork.nearestNode`, `web/arail/core/network.js`): it searched and sorted all nodes for every walk from a place without its own node, mostly the same few points (on Bf Neustadt over a thousand times a minute). The answer is kept per point and mode, as the network does not change once built: starting a trip in the town takes about a quarter of the time (on Bf Neustadt 3 ms instead of 11 ms), and the frames of 40–130 ms it caused while many people set out are gone.
- **People going to the train stayed at a platform whose tracks are both held** (`web/arail/sims/passengers.js`): the town's people, who wait for any train, stayed at a platform whose tracks were both kept for a story's trains; they now give up and go home, as the other passengers do.
