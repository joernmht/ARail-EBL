- **A simpler panel** (`web/app/panels.js`, `web/app/overview.js`, `web/app/interaction.js`): the chapter Interaction is called **Examples**, without a second heading of that name inside it. **Settings → Simulation** has only presets, which show what can be set: the time of day (Morning, Noon, Evening, Night, the part of the day it is pressed), the fast clock, a day of each season (or no date), the weather and the demand (Quiet, Normal, Busy, Rush hour). Its clock face, slider, date fields, town figures, trip table and stops board are gone, as are the tracking details of Settings → View and the train table of Settings → Control system. What is measured is in **Overview → Measured**, one figure and a short note each: the weather, the town, the trips from door to door (with their CSV), the people waiting at the stops and their mood, a control system's trains, the tracking and the model wagons. **Settings → View → Layouts** (formerly Examples) lists the example layouts without the prefix "Example:".
