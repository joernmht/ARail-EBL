- **Short notice: a mission for the maintenance district manager, a week of works** (`web/game/missions/short-notice.json`, `web/arail/game/works.js`, `web/game/works.js`): Monday 06:00 to Friday 06:00 in the district around Bf Neustadt, in 32 minutes, on the lab's table by day and by night. Four works are in the plan (a tamping night at Bergheim, the inspection of two points, a rail with a known defect at Talsee, the vegetation on the branch to Waldau), and a driver's report of a hard knock, a failed point and a storm come unplanned. What it teaches is who must be told what, and when: the possession planner a day and a half ahead, the works council a day ahead and before the people, the people two days ahead, the town between three days and one, the railway operations manager within the hour of a dangerous event. Told late, they have the law on their side (a claim, the labour court, a fine, proceedings); told early, the week moves and it must be corrected; told what they do not need, they lose patience too. Five technicians do the work, each with a home (at Bergheim, in the town, at Talsee, at Waldau, 45 km away) and a week of their own (children, a mother to care for, an evening school, the fire brigade): who is put on a night, and who goes to a fault from where, follows from that. A possession by day is single-line working in dense traffic, one at night meets a train an hour and needs the night's notices. How long a possession is registered for and when a track is forecast to be open again are numbers. On the table the people are vans that drive from their homes, a work is a building site with its possession on the rails and lamps in the dark, the trains held are discs beside the track, the houses beside a night show whether they were told, the four people are pins in the colour of their patience, and in the storm the layout's weather is the storm ([How to develop the game](docs/mission-design.md#a-fourth-example-short-notice), [Games](docs/games.md#the-shift-on-the-table)).
