- **Work orders for the container terminal** (`orders` of the terminal entry, `web/arail/terminal/operations.js`): which container goes where, `{"container": "…", "to": "…"}`, to a place (a yard block, a wagon, a barge, a model wagon), a visit, a unit of model wagons (`"Truck"`) or any `yard`, `wagon`, `truck` or `barge`. `TerminalSimulation.orders()` says which are done (the container stands there), how many containers are on top of each and which are on their way; the stage outlines the containers of the open ones in Orange with their number and where they go, also deep in a stack; the validation reports orders for containers that are not in the list, twice for one container, or to a target there is not. The case Terminal lists them above the containers (**Work orders**): choosing one chooses its container with the order's place first in **Move to**, and **Move** follows the orders, so that a keyboard does not pass the whole list; a container that is buried offers the one at the top of its stack (**Choose … on top first**). See [Container terminal](docs/container-terminal.md#the-loading-task).
- **The loading task at the hybrid container train** (module **Loading task** of `web/layouts/ebl-container-train.json`, `app/?example=container-task`): the terminal full, Block A's stacks at most three high with a container in every bay and the 40 ft stacks full, the train full but for two spots; six work orders: three containers onto the real container train (one from the bottom of a stack, one from under another, one from the real truck), the 40 ft high cube off it into the yard, where it has no place until the crane clears two bays side by side, and two containers onto the real truck, whose two spots are taken by one. The scenario **Load the hybrid train** plays it and counts the time on the terminal's clock and the moves; the best is 11 moves, about 19 minutes.
