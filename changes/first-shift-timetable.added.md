- **A timetable, windows to place, and a dispatcher who calls only when it matters** (`web/game/missions/first-shift.json`, `web/game/windows.js`, `web/arail/game/signaller.js`, `web/arail/game/mission.js`): in *Your first shift* the train dispatcher talks you through the first trains; then you work by the timetable, a window on the stage with every train's track, arrival, departure or passing, note, where it is and how late, and the dispatcher calls only when something does not go to plan (a train standing at a signal, leaving late, going to another track, a connection missed). A mission's story can now answer such irregularities (a line `on` a kind a game reports, `Game.irregular`), and the panel and the timetable can be moved and sized by the player, with the pointer or the arrow keys; where they were left is kept in the browser ([the play page](docs/games.md), [missions as data](docs/game-core.md#missions-as-data)).
