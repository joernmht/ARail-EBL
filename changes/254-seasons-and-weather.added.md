- **A date on the clock** (`clock.date`, Settings → Simulation → Date and weather, a scenario's `set.date`; [Seasons and weather](docs/weather.md)): with its year and weekday (the clock face shows *Sunday, 6 December 2026*, the clock on the stage *Sun 6 Dec · 15:46 · −2 °C · snow*), leap years and summer time. The sun rises and sets as on that day in Dresden (08:06 to 16:01 on 21 December), the date runs on at midnight, and the trees follow the season: bare, leafing, in leaf, coloured, falling. Without a date everything is as before.
- **Weather** (`world.weather`, `web/arail/core/weather.js`; the base of the concept in #254): off by default, typical for the date (Dresden's climate, the same for the same seed and date), or fixed (clear, cloudy, overcast, rain, heavy rain, thunderstorm, snowfall, frost, fog, heat), with courses over the day and a ground that remembers wet, snow and ice. The rails take their condition from it (wet, leaves, ice), people on foot are slower on snow and ice, fewer cyclists ride in the cold and in rain, and the market has fewer visitors in rain and sells more hot drinks in the cold. In the picture: snow covers the whole layout (the ground, the roofs, a fir's tiers) until warmth melts it or the traffic clears the streets; wet streets are darker; a veil of cloud and fog; rain and soft flakes falling in three layers of pre-painted tiles (one fill per layer and frame); thunderstorms with a short bolt now and then (never more than two flashes a second); with reduced motion nothing moves or flashes. The Wintermarkt's evening is now 6 December at dusk, in snow.
