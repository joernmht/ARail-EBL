- **A date on the clock** (`clock.date`, Settings → Simulation → Date and weather, a scenario's `set.date`; [Seasons and weather](docs/weather.md)): the sun rises and sets as on that day in Dresden (08:06 to 16:01 on 21 December), the date runs on at midnight, and the trees follow the season: bare, leafing, in leaf, coloured, falling. Without a date everything is as before.
- **Weather** (`world.weather`, `web/arail/core/weather.js`; the base of the concept in #254): off by default, typical for the date (Dresden's climate, the same for the same seed and date), or fixed (clear, cloudy, overcast, rain, heavy rain, snowfall, frost, fog, heat), with courses over the day and a ground that remembers wet, snow and ice. The rails take their condition from it (wet, leaves, ice), people on foot are slower on snow and ice, fewer cyclists ride in the cold and in rain, and the market has fewer visitors in rain and sells more hot drinks in the cold. In the picture: snow cover on the ground and the roofs, wet streets, a veil of cloud and fog, rain and flakes falling (standing still with reduced motion). The Striezelmarkt's evening is now 6 December at dusk, in snow.
