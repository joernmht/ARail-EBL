- **Hump a train** (Interaction, Railway undertaking and Freight; `web/app/hump.js`, `HumpYardObject.startTask`): you are the hump master of Bf Neustadt's hump yard. Start switches its module on, empties the classification tracks and puts a train of its own on the push track; you push it slower or faster, stop it before the crest and push on, and follow every cut from the hump into its track (choose one in the list or tap its wagon in the flyover). Pushed too fast, cuts go into the wrong track or meet the wagons too hard; the task counts the time on the yard's clock, the cuts in the wrong track and the impacts too hard. See [Getting started](docs/getting-started.md#hump-a-train-the-hump-yard) and [Hump yard](docs/hump-yard.md#in-the-app-hump-a-train).
- **Example: hump yard (virtual)** in Settings → View → Examples: Bf Neustadt with its module Hump yard, Hump a train shown in Interaction.
