- **The snow on the streets cleared by each day's own traffic** (`Weather.settle`, `web/arail/core/weather.js`, [#266](https://github.com/joernmht/ARail-EBL/issues/266)): the typical weather goes through the day and a half before the time set, and the traffic clears the snow it leaves; every day of it had today's traffic, so on a Monday morning the Sunday before had the rush hours of a working day. Each day now has its own: a weekend's on a Saturday and a Sunday.
- **A situation is pressed only while it holds** (Settings → Simulation, `web/app/situations.js`, `web/app/panels.js`, [#266](https://github.com/joernmht/ARail-EBL/issues/266)): a scenario, a story or a plugin that set the time, the date, the weather or the demand left the situation's card pressed (*Spring Sunday* after a scenario set 06:30 on a working day). It is pressed while nothing has set the time or the date since, the weather and the demand are as it set them and the layout is the same; the clock running on and the weather changing by itself leave it pressed.
- **A situation that does not fit changes nothing** (`situationProblem`, `web/app/situations.js`, [#266](https://github.com/joernmht/ARail-EBL/issues/266)): its date, time, weather, demand and crowd are checked before anything is set, also before its modules are switched on; a failure said "could not be set" when the modules were already switched and the crowd before had ended. The reasons are in German too.
- **A scenario's "02-29" is decided by the year it runs in** (`dateProblem`, `web/arail/core/clock.js`, `web/arail/core/layout.js`, `web/arail/core/scenarios.js`, [#261](https://github.com/joernmht/ARail-EBL/issues/261)): the layout check judged a step's `"02-29"` by the layout's year, while the step runs in the clock's year then, which Settings, an earlier step or New Year may have changed; a scenario that set 2028 and then `"02-29"` was reported though it runs. The check now takes it (it is a date in a leap year), and in a year without 29 February the step fails and names the year: *"02-29" is no date in 2027, which has no 29 February*. A layout's own `clock.date: "02-29"` is reported with its year, 2026. A step that fails no longer sets its demand and speed either.
- **The ground set by hand in the typical weather** (`Weather.settle`, `web/arail/core/weather.js`, [#261](https://github.com/joernmht/ARail-EBL/issues/261)): `wet` and `snow_cm` (a layout's `weather.state`, or with a weather a scenario sets) counted only for a fixed weather; `{"mode": "typical", "state": {"snow_cm": 10}}` started without snow, as [Seasons and weather](docs/weather.md) promised otherwise. They now lie over what the day and a half of the typical weather or a course leaves; with mode `off` the layout check says they do nothing.
- **The Overview goes through the town's trips only when one has ended** (`Overview._tripStats`, `web/app/overview.js`, [#263](https://github.com/joernmht/ARail-EBL/issues/263)): its figures, every 400 ms while it is open, went through the town's whole log of trips (up to 5000) each time for *Door to door*; they are kept now until a trip ends.
- **Start all again says what it sets back** (Railway undertaking → Journeys, `web/game/journeys.js`, [#263](https://github.com/joernmht/ARail-EBL/issues/263)): setting the clock back to before the first departure sets the rest of the simulation back too (the town's people, the market's visitors and the weather are as at that time); the message says so now, in German too.
- **Market visitors without a market say so** (`MarketSimulation._noMarket`, `web/arail/sims/market.js`, [#253](https://github.com/joernmht/ARail-EBL/issues/253)): with the module *Market visitors* on and the *Wintermarkt* off, the square has no stalls and no rides, and nobody came without a word, also in the scenario *Closing time at the Wintermarkt*. While the market is open, the visitors now say once that no market square has stalls or rides, so nobody comes.
