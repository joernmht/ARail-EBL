- **The snow on the streets cleared by each day's own traffic** (`Weather.settle`, `web/arail/core/weather.js`, [#266](https://github.com/joernmht/ARail-EBL/issues/266)): the typical weather goes through the day and a half before the time set, and the traffic clears the snow it leaves; every day of it had today's traffic, so on a Monday morning the Sunday before had the rush hours of a working day. Each day now has its own: a weekend's on a Saturday and a Sunday.
- **A situation is pressed only while it holds** (Settings → Simulation, `web/app/situations.js`, `web/app/panels.js`, [#266](https://github.com/joernmht/ARail-EBL/issues/266)): a scenario, a story or a plugin that set the time, the date, the weather or the demand left the situation's card pressed (*Spring Sunday* after a scenario set 06:30 on a working day). It is pressed while nothing has set the time or the date since, the weather and the demand are as it set them and the layout is the same; the clock running on and the weather changing by itself leave it pressed.
- **A situation that does not fit changes nothing** (`situationProblem`, `web/app/situations.js`, [#266](https://github.com/joernmht/ARail-EBL/issues/266)): its date, time, weather, demand and crowd are checked before anything is set, also before its modules are switched on; a failure said "could not be set" when the modules were already switched and the crowd before had ended. The reasons are in German too.
