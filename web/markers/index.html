Sticker sheet

  1. Print one test page on plain paper (Test print), at 100 % (“actual size”, not “fit to page”). Hold it against a sticker sheet in front of a light: the outlines must cover the stickers. If not, correct the measurements or the printer correction.
  2. Untick Test print, put the sticker sheet in the printer the right way up and print again at 100 %.
  3. Measure the black square with a ruler and enter it in the app (Build → Layout). Peel the stickers off and stick them flat on the layout; they may be rotated freely.
  4. Each sticker carries one container spot's tag. Stick it centred on that spot of the wagon (between its spigots), the arrow A ▶ towards the wagon's A end: spot 0 at the A end, then 1, 2, …
  5. Measure the height of the stickers above the table and enter it as height_mm of markers.rolling in the layout.
