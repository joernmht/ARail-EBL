Deck cards

One card per wagon with an AprilTag 36h11 tag on each container spot. Tag ID = (wagon − 1) × IDs per wagon + spot; spot 0 is at the A end.

  1. Print at 100 % (“actual size”, not “fit to page”).
  2. Check the 100 mm bar on the sheet with a ruler.
  3. Cut each card out along its grey outline. The arrow beside it points to the A end (spot 0).
  4. Lay or glue the card on the container spigots, centred on the wagon, with that end at the wagon's A end. One visible tag is enough to find the wagon.
  5. Measure the height of the cards above the table and enter it as height_mm of markers.rolling in the layout.
