About the catalogue

From the politician to the cleaning staff: the roles of the German railway system, each with its decisions, means and limits, personal risks, career, and a mini game. Each game works on its own, for teaching; together they make one big game, played as a career or as a railway undertaking's simulator (the game design).

Compiled for game design and teaching; names, rules and numbers are simplified and in orders of magnitude, to be checked by the chair's experts.

A career through the roles

The career mode follows one person through these roles. It is not a path laid out in advance: from a school certificate, some jobs are open and some educations can be started; many roles need an apprenticeship, a degree, a function training or a licence first, and years in other roles. Each role's card shows its ways in.
