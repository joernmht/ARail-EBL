Career mode · prototype

A career on the railway

Not a path laid out in advance: from where you stand, some jobs are open to you and some educations can be started. Many roles need an apprenticeship, a degree, a function training or a licence first, and years in other roles. Choose your next step, work on, train again, change direction.

The requirements are those of the role catalogue, simplified; in the full game every step is a round of that role's game, and applications and elections can fail.
