Signaller at Bf Neustadt

Half an hour of a morning at Bf Neustadt, worked from a relay interlocking with a track diagram panel. Set the routes from a start signal to a destination: the interlocking moves the points, locks the route and clears the signal. Keep the trains on time and the connections at platform 2; let the intercity pass the freight train. At some point a signal fails.

Simplified for teaching, after the German rules for signallers (Ril 408); the station is that of the lab and of the app's maintenance story.
